CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ticket booking, cancellations, and partner cinema policies.

How do I cancel my movie ticket or pass?

Navigate to your confirmation page or open your booking link from SMS/Email and click Cancel Booking. For supported multiplex chains (PVR INOX, Cinepolis, Miraj), cancellations requested up to 2 hours before showtime are immediately returned to your original payment method.

Can I book tickets without creating an account?

Yes! Guest checkout is fully enabled on CineBuzz. Simply provide an email and mobile number where your digital M-Ticket and QR barcode will be delivered. However, having a registered account allows you to save favorite screens and view one-click order history.

Which payment options and bank cards are supported?

We support all major Indian credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex), instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED Pay), and popular Net Banking portals. All transactions are protected via 256-bit bank-grade encryption with RBI 2-factor authentication.

Are cinema offers automatically applied at payment?

Yes. When you arrive at the checkout payment screen, our system reads your order total and ticket count against our 18 verified partner offers (HDFC BOGO, ICICI 25%, CRED Flat ₹100, etc.) and auto-suggests the highest monetary discount available.

I manage a theatre or live venue. How do we partner with CineBuzz?

We are constantly welcoming new multiplex chains, single-screen historical theatres, and live concert organizers. Drop our partnerships team a note via our Contact Page and our integration engineers will have your box-office online within 48 hours.
