Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my booking?

Open your booking from the confirmation email or the "My Bookings" section and tap Cancel. Refunds are processed to your original payment method within minutes, minus a small cancellation window fee.

Can I book tickets without an account?

Yes! Guest checkout is fully supported. However, creating an account unlocks offers, faster refunds and order history.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit & debit cards, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and popular wallets. All payments are processed over 256-bit encrypted connections.

Is there a convenience fee?

A small platform fee helps us keep the lights on. It's always shown clearly before you pay, and first-time users pay ₹0.

Do you work with cinema partners?

Absolutely. If you run a cinema and want to offer online booking, reach out via the contact page — onboarding takes under 48 hours.
